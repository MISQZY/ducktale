## Getting a whitelist pass

<Callout variant="warning" title="Required step">
  DuckBurg is a closed server. You won't be able to log in with your username without a pass.
</Callout>

You can get a pass in three ways:

- **A media personality** — ask any streamer or media figure from the project, for example [6oJIeH](https://www.twitch.tv/6ojieh).
- **A friend on the server** — a friend who already plays can run the command `/invitefriend <username>`. The command costs **500 Ducats**.
- **Staff** — reach out to a Moderator or Administrator directly.
