## Main commands

<CommandCard
  command="/t new"
  description="Found a new town. Requires starting capital in the treasury."
  usage="/t new <name>"
  permission="all"
  warning="The name can't be changed after creation — choose carefully."
/>

<CommandCard
  command="/t claim"
  description="Add the current chunk to the town's territory. The chunk must border already-claimed land."
  usage="/t claim [radius]"
  aliases={["/town claim"]}
  permission="all"
/>

<CommandCard
  command="/t set mayor"
  description="Hand mayoral rights to another player."
  usage="/t set mayor <player>"
  permission="admin"
  warning="This action is irreversible. The new mayor gets full control of the town."
/>

## Important restrictions

<Callout variant="warning" title="Building too close">
  It's forbidden to build a base **closer than 70 blocks** to someone else's territory without the owner's consent.
  Violations lead to a rollback and a warning.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="danger" title="Lag machines">
  Building constructs that intentionally strain the server is punished with a ban from 1 day up to permanent.
</Callout>
