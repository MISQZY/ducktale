**Towny** is DuckBurg's key plugin, around which the server's entire social and political life is built. Without it, everyone would be on their own: anyone could break your build, take over your territory, or destroy your farm. Towny solves this — it gives players tools to protect land, build communities, and run politics at the scale of entire nations.

In short: **a town protects your blocks, a nation protects your town**.

Full plugin documentation: [Towny Wiki](https://github.com/TownyAdvanced/Towny/wiki)

---

## Why you need Towny at all

On DuckBurg, without a town you're **homeless**. That's not an insult, it's a technical fact: blocks you place in the wilderness can be broken by any player. Protection only works on territory claimed by a town.

But Towny isn't just a lock on a door. It's a whole system where:

- every patch of land has an owner
- town residents have different access levels and rights
- towns can trade, wage war, and form alliances
- the economy runs on taxes and territory upkeep

The bigger and richer your town, the more land it controls, and the stronger its position on the server.

---

## Chunks — the basis of everything

The entire world is divided into **chunks** — vertical 16×16 block columns. Land in Towny is measured in chunks, not individual blocks.

When a mayor **claims** a chunk (`/town claim`), it comes under the town's control. This means:

- outside players **can't break or place blocks** on that chunk
- opening chests, doors, levers — also not allowed without permission
- the plot's owner within the chunk can configure access individually

Chunks aren't free. Every claimed chunk costs money from the **town treasury** — daily. If the treasury runs dry, the town won't be able to pay for its land and will eventually **disband automatically**. So watching the balance is the mayor's first duty.

### How many chunks can be claimed

At the moment we don't set any limits on town size.

## Plots inside a town

A mayor can turn claimed chunks into **plots** of different purposes and hand them out to residents or configure them specially.

| Type | Who it's for | Features |
|-----|--------------|-------------|
| **Regular** | Any resident | A standard residential plot |
| **Shop** | Merchants | Shop chests can be set up for outside players |
| **Farm** | Farmers | Outsiders can harvest crops, but not break the ground |
| **Arena** | For PvP | PvP is on by default, no item loss on death (optional) |
| **Jail** | Staff | Offenders are sent here by the mayor's command |
| **Embassy** | Diplomacy | Players from other towns can buy a plot here |
| **Outpost** | Expansion | A remote outpost, doesn't need to border the main territory |

Once a resident is **given a plot**, it becomes their personal territory within the town — even the mayor can't break blocks there without special rights.

---

## Rights and hierarchy

There's a clear hierarchy within a town:

**Mayor** — the sole owner of the town. Can do everything: claim land, kick residents, disband the town, found a nation.

**Assistants** — players with expanded rights, appointed by the mayor. Can invite residents, manage plots, and deposit into the treasury.

**Residents** — regular town members. Protected from griefing by default, but have no town management rights.

The mayor can create **custom ranks** with any set of rights and assign them to residents. For example: "builder" with permission to claim land, "banker" with permission to deposit into the treasury, "guard" with permission to jail people.

---

## Nations and politics

Once a town reaches a certain size and influence, its mayor can found a **nation** — a union of towns under a single flag. The capital's mayor becomes **king**.

Other towns can **join the nation** — voluntarily or by invitation. This gives them:

- protection from the nation's enemies
- access to allied towns' territory
- political weight

Nations can:

- form **alliances** with other nations — allied residents see each other as "friendly"
- declare **enemies** — wars are possible between hostile nations
- set **taxes** for member towns
- declare **neutrality** — protection from wars for an extra fee

Server politics runs at the nation level — blocs of alliances, standoffs, trade deals: that's all real server life.

---

## The wilderness and its dangers

Territory that no town has claimed is the **wilderness**. There's no protection here at all: anyone can break blocks, open chests, and build nearby.

If you build a base in the wilderness, you're doing it at your own risk. The only way to protect yourself is to claim the land as part of a town.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Building near someone else's territory">
  It's forbidden to build a base closer than **70 blocks** to someone else's claimed territory without the owner's consent. Violations are punished with a build rollback and a warning. Before you settle somewhere, check the map with `/towny map` or the <strong>World Map</strong>.
</Callout>
