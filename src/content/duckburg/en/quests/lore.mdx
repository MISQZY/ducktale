description: Waking up in the world of DuckBurg and the road to Goldenburg
---

You come to not quite yourself — a digital imprint, raised from the archives of a long-forgotten civilization. The transfer went off without a hitch: limbs in place, no extra pair of eyes. Around you, other imprints are stirring too — you can spot them by the green bar over their heads in the player list.

The Instructor is the first to greet you. From him you learn where you've ended up: the capital of the Duck nation — once a thriving civilization that fell into decline for reasons still unknown. He doesn't hand you easy answers — instead, he sends you off to where every story of rebuilding a civilization truly begins: the trade square.

He gives you a letter for the mayor of Goldenburg — the world of DuckBurg's largest trading hub — and leaves a note with the coordinates in your backpack. From here, the road continues without him: what waits in Goldenburg, and beyond it, is yours to discover.
