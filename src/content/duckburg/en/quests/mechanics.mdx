---
title: Mechanics
description: Goldenburg's mayor and merchant teach how the world of DuckBurg works
---

In Goldenburg you're met by the town's mayor, who runs newcomers through a quick introduction and is happy to answer a couple of questions about his domain. From there the road splits in two, and neither branch has to come before the other.

The first leads to the town's merchant, who explains how DuckBurg's economy works — ducats, the world's shared currency, and the tower by the square where diamonds get exchanged for your first capital. It's an optional stop for anyone who wants to understand trade before moving on.

The second is what actually makes leaving Goldenburg worthwhile: the mayor points the way to Karda, the place where real city-building is taught. That's where everything tied to Towny begins — from claiming your first plot to building a town of your own.
