## Farm status by distance from spawn

| Distance from spawn | Status | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **< 2000 blocks** | 🟡 Public | Staff may modify or demolish the farm if necessary |
| **> 2000 blocks** | 🟢 Private | The owner sets the restrictions |

<Callout variant="info">
  Distance is calculated from the center of world spawn on the XZ plane. Use `/t spawn` or the [map](/maps/duckburg) to orient yourself.
</Callout>
