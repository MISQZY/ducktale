Rules exist to keep the game fair and enjoyable for everyone. Not knowing the rules doesn't exempt you from responsibility.

## Principles

- Treat other players with respect and avoid conflicts.
- Help newcomers — everyone was new once.
- Play fair: no cheats, bug abuse, or banned modifications.
