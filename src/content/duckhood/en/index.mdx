DuckHood is a creative server focused on calmly building projects to later show off to friends or move over to the survival server.
#  Core concepts of the server

- **Personal plots** — safely build your projects without fear of losing them to griefing
- **Accessibility** — WorldEdit is available within your own plot, along with gamemode-switching and teleport-to-player commands

# How to play on the server
Use the sidebar menu to learn all the server's mechanics. Once you've gone through the "Getting Started" section, you'll be able to join the server with your friends without any trouble.
